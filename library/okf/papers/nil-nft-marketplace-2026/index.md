# Paper Summary

The paper designs a fair and inclusive digital-asset marketplace for NIL monetization. It derives design requirements from student-athlete interviews and market-design theory, proposes a randomized primary-market and royalty-based secondary-market NFT mechanism, implements a DApp prototype, evaluates the artifact with stakeholders, and formulates an NIL design theory.
