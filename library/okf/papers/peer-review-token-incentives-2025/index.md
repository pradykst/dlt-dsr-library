# Paper Summary

The paper develops a blockchain-based token system for incentivizing peer review. It identifies persistent reviewer-recruitment problems, derives design principles around incentives, flexibility, and trust, implements these principles through tokenization, immutability, and decentralization, demonstrates a tokenized peer-review platform, and evaluates the artifact through cost analysis, survey data, and interviews.
