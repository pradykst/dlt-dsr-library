# Certified data chats for future used car markets

**Authors:** Andreas Engelmann, Gerhard Schwabe  
**Venue:** Electronic Markets 34:45 (2024)  
**Link:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s12525-024-00725-z

## Summary

The study designs a certified data chat that integrates blockchain-based certified car data into negotiation, guided by three design goals and three design principles (certified data provision, selective information disclosure, and data presentation as overview and within conversation), and evaluates it in an experimental market game.

## Artifact

A certified data chat for selective, controlled exchange of blockchain-based certified data in used-car negotiations.

## Methodology

Design science research; design and development of a chat artifact; experimental market game with students.

## DSR grid

The six core dimensions of this DSR project, following the [DSR grid](../about-the-dsr-grid.md) (vom Brocke & Maedche, 2019):

* **Problem description.** Extending the used-car customer journey onto a platform requires designing market communication and negotiation when blockchain-based certified data comes into play.
* **Input knowledge.** The certified-data concept; the market-for-lemons and information-asymmetry literature; negotiation and instant-messaging research; the anatomy of a design principle (Gregor et al. 2020).
* **Research process.** Design science research: design and development of a certified data chat, evaluated with an experimental market game played by students.
* **Key concepts.** Certified data, future used car market, blockchain, data market, chat.
* **Solution description.** A certified data chat for selective, controlled exchange of blockchain-certified car data in used-car negotiations. Solution-space representation: Instantiation (chat artifact) plus design goals and design principles (nascent design theory).
* **Output knowledge.** Three design goals and three design principles for certified-data chat integration.

The *output knowledge* of this project is captured as the atomic design-knowledge concepts listed below.

## Design knowledge

* [Design goal DG1: Useful integration of certified data assets](../design-knowledge/certified-data-chats-used-cars-dg1.md) - Implement a useful integration of certified data assets into the chat.
* [Design goal DG2: Simple and seamless interaction](../design-knowledge/certified-data-chats-used-cars-dg2.md) - Enable simple and seamless interaction using the certified data assets.
* [Design goal DG3: Transparent and manageable disclosure](../design-knowledge/certified-data-chats-used-cars-dg3.md) - Enable transparent and manageable disclosure using the certified data assets.
* [Design principle DP1: Certified data provision](../design-knowledge/certified-data-chats-used-cars-dp1.md) - Provide certified data to negotiating sellers and buyers in a certified data area, so that both parties can consider the car data credible and trustworthy, increasing market transparency.
* [Design principle DP2: Selective information disclosure](../design-knowledge/certified-data-chats-used-cars-dp2.md) - Allow negotiating sellers to selectively disclose information to the buyers by moving certified data to the conversation area (e.g., via drag-and-drop), enabling context-individual disclosure manag...
* [Design principle DP3: Data presentation as overview and within conversation](../design-knowledge/certified-data-chats-used-cars-dp3.md) - Present certified data in used-car negotiations both as an overview and within the conversation, marking certified messages so they are recognized as certified and tamper-proof.
