# Blockchain to Rule the Waves - Nascent Design Principles for Reducing Risk and Uncertainty in Decentralized Environments

**Authors:** Kristoffer Naerland, Christoph Mueller-Bloch, Roman Beck, Soren Palmund  
**Venue:** ICIS 2017 Proceedings  
**Link:** https://aisel.aisnet.org/icis2017/HCI/Presentations/12

## Summary

Through a bill-of-lading use case, the paper presents first evidence for four nascent design principles - digitization, tamper-proof storage, accessibility, and user authentication - that mitigate transactional risk and uncertainty in decentralized environments.

## Artifact

A blockchain prototype (bill of lading) for reducing transactional risk and uncertainty in international trade.

## Methodology

Design science research; use-case-driven prototype; industry-expert feedback; theory generation.

## DSR grid

The six core dimensions of this DSR project, following the [DSR grid](../about-the-dsr-grid.md) (vom Brocke & Maedche, 2019):

* **Problem description.** Reducing transactional risk and uncertainty in decentralized international-trade environments, illustrated with a bill-of-lading use case.
* **Input knowledge.** Simon's sciences of the artificial (problem-solving); the existing knowledge base and data; blockchain - explicitly deriving nascent insights rather than following a single kernel theory.
* **Research process.** Design science research: a use-case-driven prototype, industry-expert feedback, and theory generation (empirical grounding of nascent principles).
* **Key concepts.** Blockchain, bill of lading, international trade, decentralized environments.
* **Solution description.** A blockchain bill-of-lading prototype embodying four nascent design principles. Solution-space representation: Instantiation (prototype) plus nascent design principles.
* **Output knowledge.** Four nascent design principles: digitization, tamper-proof storage, accessibility and user authentication.

The *output knowledge* of this project is captured as the atomic design-knowledge concepts listed below.

## Design knowledge

* [Design principle DP1: Digitization](../design-knowledge/rule-the-waves-shipping-dp1.md) - All data is stored and exchanged digitally, reducing the likelihood of data loss and enabling faster, more cost-effective information exchange as well as the use of blockchain technology.
* [Design principle DP2: Tamper-proof storage](../design-knowledge/rule-the-waves-shipping-dp2.md) - All changes made to the data stored in the system can be retraced, so that information cannot be lost and (together with user authentication) all changes can be traced to identifiable users.
* [Design principle DP3: Accessibility](../design-knowledge/rule-the-waves-shipping-dp3.md) - The system can be accessed easily even by technically non-sophisticated stakeholders.
* [Design principle DP4: User authentication](../design-knowledge/rule-the-waves-shipping-dp4.md) - All activities in the system can be traced back to certain users.
