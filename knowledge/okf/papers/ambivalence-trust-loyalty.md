# From ambivalence to trust: Using blockchain in customer loyalty programs

**Authors:** Manuel Utz, Simon Johanning, Tamara Roth, Thomas Bruckner, Jens Strueker  
**Venue:** International Journal of Information Management 68 (2023) 102496  
**Link:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijinfomgt.2022.102496

## Summary

To reduce customer ambivalence toward green electricity tariffs, the paper develops a blockchain-based loyalty program refined over four iterations, structured around six design objectives (authenticity, customizability, transparency, efficiency, usability and affordability) with associated design requirements.

## Artifact

A blockchain-based customer loyalty program for green electricity tariffs.

## Methodology

Design science research; four iterations; workshop and 18 ex-ante expert interviews.

## DSR grid

The six core dimensions of this DSR project, following the [DSR grid](../about-the-dsr-grid.md) (vom Brocke & Maedche, 2019):

* **Problem description.** Customers of green electricity tariffs feel ambivalence - lost trust plus distrust from information asymmetries and unmet 'green' promises - which may lead them to switch suppliers.
* **Input knowledge.** Theories of trust, distrust and ambivalence; customer-loyalty literature; kernel-theory-to-artifact relations (Walls et al.); blockchain and IoT.
* **Research process.** Design science research refined over four iterations, with a workshop and eighteen ex-ante expert interviews.
* **Key concepts.** Ambivalence, blockchain, customer loyalty, design science research, distrust, trust.
* **Solution description.** A blockchain-based customer loyalty program for green electricity tariffs using loyalty tokens and local IoT controllers. Solution-space representation: Instantiation (program/prototype) plus design objectives and requirements (nascent design theory).
* **Output knowledge.** Six design objectives (authenticity, customizability, transparency, efficiency, usability, affordability) with underlying design requirements.

The *output knowledge* of this project is captured as the atomic design-knowledge concepts listed below.

## Design knowledge

* [Design objective DO1: Authenticity](../design-knowledge/ambivalence-trust-loyalty-do1.md) - Provide loyalty tokens and data from authentic sources - e.g., using local IoT controllers for consumption data and a green/renewable energy index for generation - so customers can trust the sustai...
* [Design objective DO2: Customizability](../design-knowledge/ambivalence-trust-loyalty-do2.md) - Let customers set rules for their smart appliances based on generation and consumption data so that electricity consumption aligns with their sustainability preferences.
* [Design objective DO3: Transparency](../design-knowledge/ambivalence-trust-loyalty-do3.md) - Assess and communicate the sustainability of generation (e.g., via a renewable energy index) so customers can transparently monitor their green electricity.
* [Design objective DO4: Efficiency](../design-knowledge/ambivalence-trust-loyalty-do4.md) - Enable seamless, reliable and scalable information exchange between electricity supplier and customer, minimizing blockchain data processing to retain high uptime and availability.
* [Design objective DO5: Usability](../design-knowledge/ambivalence-trust-loyalty-do5.md) - Provide an intuitive user interface (with a cloud controller for data storage/display) so users need not deal with the technical details of blockchain.
* [Design objective DO6: Affordability](../design-knowledge/ambivalence-trust-loyalty-do6.md) - Keep participation in the blockchain-based loyalty program affordable for customers, keeping additional-service and hardware costs (e.g., low-cost local controllers) reasonable.
