# Cross-Organizational Workflow Management Using Blockchain Technology - Towards Applicability, Auditability, and Automation

**Authors:** Gilbert Fridgen, Nils Urbach, Sven Radszuwill, Lena Utz  
**Venue:** HICSS 51 (2018)  
**Link:** https://hdl.handle.net/10125/50503

## Summary

The paper derives design objectives for a blockchain-based documentary-workflow (BDW) prototype and implements them on a private Ethereum chain, demonstrating applicability, auditability and automation of a cross-organizational trade process.

## Artifact

A blockchain-based cross-organizational workflow management prototype for documentary trade (letter of credit).

## Methodology

Design science research; case-based derivation of design objectives; private Ethereum prototype.

## DSR grid

The six core dimensions of this DSR project, following the [DSR grid](../about-the-dsr-grid.md) (vom Brocke & Maedche, 2019):

* **Problem description.** Cross-organizational documentary workflows (e.g., letters of credit) are paper-based, slow, costly and low-trust, and central workflow governance is impractical across organizations.
* **Input knowledge.** Business-process/workflow-management-system literature; documentary-trade domain knowledge; blockchain and smart contracts.
* **Research process.** Design science research: case-based derivation of design objectives from areas of improvement, followed by a private-Ethereum prototype.
* **Key concepts.** Cross-organizational workflow management, blockchain, applicability, auditability, automation.
* **Solution description.** A blockchain documentary-workflow (BDW) prototype implemented on a private Ethereum chain. Solution-space representation: Instantiation (prototype) driven by design objectives (model / nascent design knowledge).
* **Output knowledge.** Nine design objectives for the blockchain documentary-workflow prototype.
