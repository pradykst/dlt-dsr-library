# Unchaining Social Businesses - Blockchain as the Basic Technology of a Crowdlending Platform

**Authors:** Andre Schweizer, Vincent Schlatt, Nils Urbach, Gilbert Fridgen  
**Venue:** ICIS 2017 Proceedings  
**Link:** https://aisel.aisnet.org/icis2017/TransformingSociety/Presentations/8

## Summary

Studying a crowdlending social business, the paper derives 17 objectives for a blockchain-based crowdlending prototype and evaluates the prototype against a non-blockchain solution, deriving generic knowledge on blockchain applications for social business.

## Artifact

A blockchain-based crowdlending platform prototype for social businesses.

## Methodology

Design science research; case study of a crowdlending social business; 17 solution objectives; prototype and evaluation vs. non-blockchain solution.

## DSR grid

The six core dimensions of this DSR project, following the [DSR grid](../about-the-dsr-grid.md) (vom Brocke & Maedche, 2019):

* **Problem description.** Social businesses face conflicting objectives (social purpose versus competitive advantage and financial sustainability), and crowdlending platforms need trustworthy, low-cost infrastructure.
* **Input knowledge.** The Peffers et al. DSR process; social-business and crowdlending literature; market-design parameters; blockchain and smart contracts.
* **Research process.** Design science research (Peffers et al.): a case study with a founder interview, a prototype, and evaluation against a non-blockchain solution.
* **Key concepts.** Blockchain, social business, smart contracts, crowdlending, design science.
* **Solution description.** A blockchain-based crowdlending platform prototype for social businesses. Solution-space representation: Instantiation (prototype) driven by seventeen design objectives (model / nascent design knowledge).
* **Output knowledge.** Seventeen objectives for the blockchain crowdlending prototype, plus generic knowledge on blockchain for social business.
