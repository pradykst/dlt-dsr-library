# Design principle DP3: Traceability with customer notifications

Enable traceability functions with customer wallet notifications and backward and forward ownership tracing to ensure synchronized communication for all related owners.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [From Dissonance to Dialogue: A Token-Based Approach to Bridge the Gap Between Manufacturers and Customers](../papers/dissonance-dialogue-recall.md) (Norman Pytel, Christian Ziegler, Axel Winkelmann, 2024).
