# Design principle DP1: Standardized representation of traceability objects

Provide a standardized representation of traceability objects through a hybrid, token-based traceability system, with organizations collaboratively determining necessary dimensions before developing concrete features.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Digging for Quality Management in Production Systems: A Solution Space for Blockchain Collaborations](../papers/quality-management-production.md) (Norman Pytel, Benedikt Putz, Fabian Boehm, Axel Winkelmann, 2022).
