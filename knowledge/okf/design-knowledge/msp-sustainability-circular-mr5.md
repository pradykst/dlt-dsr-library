# Meta-requirement MR5: Data and information sharing

The platform's decentralized design should enable data and information sharing in a transparent fashion across sectors, fostering collaboration and trust.

## Source paper

This meta-requirement is proposed by [Meta-requirements for the Design of a Blockchain-enabled Multi-sided Platform for Sustainability and Circular Economy](../papers/msp-sustainability-circular.md) (Hanna Buyssens, 2024).
