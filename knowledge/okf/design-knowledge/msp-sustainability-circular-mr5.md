# Meta-requirement MR5: Data and information sharing

Blockchain technology must exchange data and information in a transparent fashion to establish a sense of shared sustainable responsibility across the platform.

## Source paper

This meta-requirement is proposed by [Meta-requirements for the Design of a Blockchain-enabled Multi-sided Platform for Sustainability and Circular Economy](../papers/msp-sustainability-circular.md) (Hanna Buyssens, Stijn Viaene, 2024).

## Related meta-requirements

* [Meta-requirement MR2: Smart contracts](./msp-sustainability-circular-mr2.md) - The paper states that blockchain's encryption and decentralization "can overcome these issues by enabling the automatic sharing of tamper-proof, quality data to relevant parties (e.g., smart contracts - MR2)."
