# Design objective DO-4: Optimized information sharing

The prototype should reduce information asymmetries, thus creating transparency across permitted stakeholders and reducing risk, which ultimately leads to higher efficiency of the prototype. Depending on the authorization, the stored data should be publicly accessible, but the prototype needs to ensure investor privacy for data protection reasons and banking confidentiality.

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [Designing the future of bond markets: Reducing transaction costs through tokenization](../papers/bond-markets-tokenization-tac.md) (David Cisar, Benjamin Schellinger, Jens-Christian Stoetzer, Vincent Gramlich, Tobias Guggenberger, Nils Urbach, Florian Lennart Weiss, 2025).
