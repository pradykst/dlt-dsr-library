# Design requirement DR4: Compliance

The solution must have the flexibility to comply with the variable body of federal and state regulatory rules governing patient consent for PHI sharing, since healthcare providers must comply with these regulations for the patient data they manage.

## Source paper

This design requirement is proposed by [Blockchain innovation for consent self-management in health information exchanges](../papers/consent-self-management-hie.md) (Chad Anderson, Arthur Carvalho, Mala Kaul, Jeffrey W. Merhout, 2023).
