# Design objective DO1: Financial sustainability

Since the social business is not based on donations, it must support the recovery of costs (Yunus et al. 2010). Thus, a balanced profit equation between cost and revenue is needed (Boons and Lüdeke-Freund 2013). Further, the startup's management indicated that, owing to their long-term commitment to investors and students, financial sustainability is necessary.

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [Unchaining Social Businesses - Blockchain as the Basic Technology of a Crowdlending Platform](../papers/unchaining-social-crowdlending.md) (Andre Schweizer, Vincent Schlatt, Nils Urbach, Gilbert Fridgen, 2017).
