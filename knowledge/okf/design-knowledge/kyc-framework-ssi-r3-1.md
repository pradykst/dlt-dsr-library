# Design requirement R3.1: Avoid central storage of customer data

Recent data breaches that revealed sensitive customer data stored in central data silos have significantly reduced confidence in their respective architectures. To avoid comparable data breaches, a viable solution for an improved eKYC process must therefore avoid central storage of customer data.

## Source paper

This design requirement is proposed by [Designing a Framework for Digital KYC Processes Built on Blockchain-Based Self-Sovereign Identity](../papers/kyc-framework-ssi.md) (Vincent Schlatt, Johannes Sedlmeir, Simon Feulner, Nils Urbach, 2022).
