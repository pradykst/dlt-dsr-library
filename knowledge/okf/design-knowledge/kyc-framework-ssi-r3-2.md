# Design requirement R3.2: Prevent lock-in effects

Banks do not want to risk becoming dependent on a centralized eKYC service provider. Thus, the system must be constructed to prevent lock-in effects that could result in the aggregation of market power.

## Source paper

This design requirement is proposed by [Designing a Framework for Digital KYC Processes Built on Blockchain-Based Self-Sovereign Identity](../papers/kyc-framework-ssi.md) (Vincent Schlatt, Johannes Sedlmeir, Simon Feulner, Nils Urbach, 2022).
