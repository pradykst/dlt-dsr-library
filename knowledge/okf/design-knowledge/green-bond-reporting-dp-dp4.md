# Design principle DP4: Reputation-based incentives

Design for reputation rather than monetary incentives.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Design Principles for Blockchain-based Applications in Green Bond Reporting](../papers/green-bond-reporting-dp.md) (Ameera Darwish, Juho Lindman, Jesper Hjertqvist, Olgerta Tona, 2023).

[2] Source document: Design Principles for Blockchain-based Applications in Green Bond.pdf
[3] Source evidence: Section 4 ("Design Principles for Green Bond Reporting"), article p. 5191: "The design principle for incentives is as follows: DP4: Design for reputation rather than monetary incentives." Table 3 ("Revised design principles"), article p. 5192, expands the revised wording to add "this incurs incentivizing proper behavior from the different stakeholders through credit-based systems and track records rather than cryptocurrency rewards" - the DP4 statement here retains the formally numbered Section 4 wording.
