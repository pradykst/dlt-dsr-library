# Design objective DO2: Collaboration

Support the collaboration aspect of coopetition, using network effects (e.g., Hyperledger Fabric) to enhance the overall construction planning process between participants.

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [Overcoming the Data Transparency Trade-Off: Designing a Blockchain-Based Delivery Invoice System for the Construction Industry](../papers/delivery-invoice-transparency.md) (Jonathan Lautenschlager, Jan Stramm, Tobias Guggenberger, Artur Roesch, Andre Schweizer, 2023).
