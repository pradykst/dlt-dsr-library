# Design principle DP6: Co-value creation between manufacturers and customers

Foster co-value creation procedures between manufacturers and customers using inter-organizational data storage, product-defect announcements, recall-state management and ownership tracing.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [From Dissonance to Dialogue: A Token-Based Approach to Bridge the Gap Between Manufacturers and Customers](../papers/dissonance-dialogue-recall.md) (Norman Pytel, Christian Ziegler, Axel Winkelmann, 2024).
