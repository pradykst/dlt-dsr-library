# Design feature DF3: Recall tracing and product-state extension of the token

A standard token interface that traces recalls and extends the product state as the token moves between organizations and customers.

## Source paper

This design feature is a technology-specific realization proposed by [From Dissonance to Dialogue: A Token-Based Approach to Bridge the Gap Between Manufacturers and Customers](../papers/dissonance-dialogue-recall.md) (Norman Pytel, Christian Ziegler, Axel Winkelmann, 2024).
