# Design principle DP2: Extended quality control over communication and objects

Provide information about direct and indirect objects to give extended quality control over communication processes, affected objects, and involved quality trading partners.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Digging for Quality Management in Production Systems: A Solution Space for Blockchain Collaborations](../papers/quality-management-production.md) (Norman Pytel, Benedikt Putz, Fabian Boehm, Axel Winkelmann, 2022).
