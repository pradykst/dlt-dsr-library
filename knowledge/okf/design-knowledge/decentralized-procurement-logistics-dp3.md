# Design principle DP3: Metrics privacy

Keep sensitive allocation metrics off-chain in competitive markets so that private data is not exposed while public allocation results remain verifiable.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Decentralized Procurement Mechanisms for Efficient Logistics Services Mapping - a Design Science Research Approach](../papers/decentralized-procurement-logistics.md) (Tiphaine Henry, Roman Beck, Walid Gaaloul, 2022).
