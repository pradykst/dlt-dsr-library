# Design principle DP3: Metrics privacy

The allocation mechanism must keep sensitive data off-chain in competitive markets.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Decentralized Procurement Mechanisms for Efficient Logistics Services Mapping - a Design Science Research Approach](../papers/decentralized-procurement-logistics.md) (Tiphaine Henry, Roman Beck, Nassim Laga, Walid Gaaloul, Shenle Pan, 2022).
