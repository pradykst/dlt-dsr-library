# Design principle DP2: Multi-enterprise capture and historical owner analysis

Capture multiple enterprise systems and analyze historical owners using blockchain-ERP integration and recall tracing, fostering cross-organizational collaboration in recall processes.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [From Dissonance to Dialogue: A Token-Based Approach to Bridge the Gap Between Manufacturers and Customers](../papers/dissonance-dialogue-recall.md) (Norman Pytel, Christian Ziegler, Axel Winkelmann, 2024).
