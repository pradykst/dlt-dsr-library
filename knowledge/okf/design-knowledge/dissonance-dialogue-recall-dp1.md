# Design principle DP1: ERP integration by extending data models

Integrate any ERP system by extending existing data models of required blockchain objects and recall tracing, promoting seamless communication between ERP and blockchain systems.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [From Dissonance to Dialogue: A Token-Based Approach to Bridge the Gap Between Manufacturers and Customers](../papers/dissonance-dialogue-recall.md) (Norman Pytel, Christian Ziegler, Axel Winkelmann, 2024).
