# Design requirement R3: Real-time payment

A solution must enact real-time payment to help carriers optimize their commercial processes and operating costs.

## Source paper

This design requirement is proposed by [Decentralized Procurement Mechanisms for Efficient Logistics Services Mapping - a Design Science Research Approach](../papers/decentralized-procurement-logistics.md) (Tiphaine Henry, Roman Beck, Nassim Laga, Walid Gaaloul, Shenle Pan, 2022).

## Addresses

* [Design objective P2: Contractual flexibility](./decentralized-procurement-logistics-do2.md) - The paper states "contractual flexibility is also related to real-time payment (cf R3)."
