# Design principle DP1: Certified data provision

Provide certified data to negotiating sellers and buyers in a certified data area, so that both parties can consider the car data credible and trustworthy, increasing market transparency.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Certified data chats for future used car markets](../papers/certified-data-chats-used-cars.md) (Andreas Engelmann, Gerhard Schwabe, 2024).
