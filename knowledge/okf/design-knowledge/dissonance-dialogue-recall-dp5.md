# Design principle DP5: Customer participation in recall information

Implement mechanisms that allow customers to receive recall information and announce product defects, promoting active customer participation.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [From Dissonance to Dialogue: A Token-Based Approach to Bridge the Gap Between Manufacturers and Customers](../papers/dissonance-dialogue-recall.md) (Norman Pytel, Christian Ziegler, Axel Winkelmann, 2024).
