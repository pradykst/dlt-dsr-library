# Design objective DO9: Provide reporting functions

For regularly reporting and statistics on the social business development, the prototype must provide the functionality to run reports on the transactions of a specific period.

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [Unchaining Social Businesses - Blockchain as the Basic Technology of a Crowdlending Platform](../papers/unchaining-social-crowdlending.md) (Andre Schweizer, Vincent Schlatt, Nils Urbach, Gilbert Fridgen, 2017).
