# Design principle DP1: Define incentives explicitly

Explicitly define a structure of incentives for interorganizational and cross-industrial blockchain applications where stakeholders' interests are not necessarily aligned.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Using Blockchain to Sustainably Manage Containers in International Shipping](../papers/containers-shipping-sustainable.md) (Roman Beck, Mikkel Boding Kildetoft, Nebojsa Radonic, 2020).
