# Design objective DO7: Data availability

Availability describes the probability that a system can provide the service at a certain point in time. Its reliability, maintainability, and redundancy typically measure a system's availability. The system should ensure constant availability.

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [Designing a cross-organizational identity management system: Utilizing SSI for the certification of retailer attributes](../papers/cross-org-identity-ssi.md) (Tobias Guggenberger, Daniela Kühne, Vincent Schlatt, Nils Urbach, 2023).
