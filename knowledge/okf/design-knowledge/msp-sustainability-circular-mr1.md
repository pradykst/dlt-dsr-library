# Meta-requirement MR1: Provenance and traceability

Blockchain must provide insights into the provenance and traceability of data to disclose the sustainable origin of products.

## Source paper

This meta-requirement is proposed by [Meta-requirements for the Design of a Blockchain-enabled Multi-sided Platform for Sustainability and Circular Economy](../papers/msp-sustainability-circular.md) (Hanna Buyssens, Stijn Viaene, 2024).

## Related meta-requirements

* [Meta-requirement MR2: Smart contracts](./msp-sustainability-circular-mr2.md) - The paper states that smart contracts "equally can facilitate increased traceability and provenance (MR1), enabling stakeholders to track and verify product origins and a product's sustainable attributes."
* [Meta-requirement MR3: Tokenization](./msp-sustainability-circular-mr3.md) - The paper states that "the tokenization process can facilitate the verification of provenance (MR1) of physical objects, thereby allowing users to examine the historical transactions associated with the asset."
