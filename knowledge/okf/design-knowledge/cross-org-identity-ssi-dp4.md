# Design principle DP4: Use public DIDs only for credential issuers to minimize privacy issues

Publish public DIDs only for credential issuers and exchange DIDs bilaterally for all other parties, minimizing on-chain transactions and privacy risks.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Designing a cross-organizational identity management system: Utilizing SSI for the certification of retailer attributes](../papers/cross-org-identity-ssi.md) (Tobias Guggenberger, Daniela Kühne, Vincent Schlatt, Nils Urbach, 2023).
