# Design principle DP2: Address environmental sustainability

Consider environmental sustainability as a non-functional requirement in the development of a blockchain artefact.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Using Blockchain to Sustainably Manage Containers in International Shipping](../papers/containers-shipping-sustainable.md) (Roman Beck, Mikkel Boding Kildetoft, Nebojsa Radonic, 2020).
