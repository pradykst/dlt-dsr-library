# Design principle DP1: Plausible events (randomized collectible sales)

Enable market-based randomized sales of royalty-paying collectibles so that every student-athlete has a non-zero chance of profiting from their NIL, satisfying the inclusiveness requirement via the possibility effect.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Designing a fair and inclusive digital asset-based name-image-likeness marketplace](../papers/nil-marketplace-fair-inclusive.md) (Arthur Carvalho, Liudmila Zavolokina, Suman Bhunia, Gerhard Schwabe, 2026).
