# Design principle DP1: Consortium blockchain

Use a consortium blockchain.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Design Principles for Blockchain-based Applications in Green Bond Reporting](../papers/green-bond-reporting-dp.md) (Ameera Darwish, Juho Lindman, Jesper Hjertqvist, Olgerta Tona, 2023).

## Related design principles

* [Design principle DP2: Proof-of-authority consensus](./green-bond-reporting-dp-dp2.md) - The paper states that "consortium blockchains are a good match for PoA consensus mechanisms because participants are known and vetted," directly linking the consortium blockchain type to the PoA consensus choice.
