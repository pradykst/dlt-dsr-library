# Design requirement DR2: Meritocratic Allocation

Relevant differences among student-athletes should be a driving factor when allocating NIL financial resources.

## Source paper

This design requirement is proposed by [Designing a fair and inclusive digital asset-based name-image-likeness marketplace](../papers/nil-marketplace-fair-inclusive.md) (Arthur Carvalho, Liudmila Zavolokina, Suman Bhunia, Gerhard Schwabe, 2026).
