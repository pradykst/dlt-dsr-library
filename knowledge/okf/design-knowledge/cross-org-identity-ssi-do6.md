# Design objective DO6: Data confidentiality

Confidentiality defines the property of data or information intended only for specific recipients. Confidentiality can be promoted or enforced by technical means. It must be ensured that data about individual parties is only accessible to them and the parties directly involved in the process.

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [Designing a cross-organizational identity management system: Utilizing SSI for the certification of retailer attributes](../papers/cross-org-identity-ssi.md) (Tobias Guggenberger, Daniela Kühne, Vincent Schlatt, Nils Urbach, 2023).
