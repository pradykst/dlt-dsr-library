# Design principle DP2: Market royalties (meritocratic allocation)

Allow ex-post, market-driven adjustments to the initial allocation through royalties from secondary-market sales of collectibles, so that student-athletes are rewarded in proportion to relevant differences, satisfying meritocratic fairness.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Designing a fair and inclusive digital asset-based name-image-likeness marketplace](../papers/nil-marketplace-fair-inclusive.md) (Arthur Carvalho, Liudmila Zavolokina, Suman Bhunia, Gerhard Schwabe, 2026).
