# Design principle DP3: Layer privacy-enhancing technologies onto private channels for complete confidentiality

Enhance private data collections and channels with additional privacy-preserving technologies to ensure complete data confidentiality.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Striking a balance: Designing a blockchain-based solution to navigate coopetition dynamics in supply chain management](../papers/striking-balance-coopetition.md) (Jonathan Lautenschlager, Jan Stramm, Tobias Guggenberger, Nils Urbach, 2025) as "Implementation Guideline 3."
