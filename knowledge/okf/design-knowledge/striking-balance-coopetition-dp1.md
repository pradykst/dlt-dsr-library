# Design principle DP1: Adopt decentralization when network effects outweigh implementation complexity

Opt for a decentralized solution when the benefits of leveraging network effects in a coopetitive market surpass the complexities and challenges of its implementation.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Striking a balance: Designing a blockchain-based solution to navigate coopetition dynamics in supply chain management](../papers/striking-balance-coopetition.md) (Jonathan Lautenschlager, Jan Stramm, Tobias Guggenberger, Nils Urbach, 2025) as "Implementation Guideline 1."
