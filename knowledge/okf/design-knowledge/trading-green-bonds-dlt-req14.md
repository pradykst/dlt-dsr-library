# Design requirement REQ14: Catalyze structured finance via a domain-specific language

Ability to catalyze structured finance by domain-specific language for specifying new instruments and immediately issuing them.

## Source paper

This design requirement is proposed by [Trading Green Bonds Using Distributed Ledger Technology](../papers/trading-green-bonds-dlt.md) (Henrik Axelsen, Ulrik Rasmussen, Johannes Rude Jensen, Omri Ross, Fritz Henglein, 2023).
