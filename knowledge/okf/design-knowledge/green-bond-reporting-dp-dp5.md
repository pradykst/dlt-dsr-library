# Design principle DP5: Off-chain decision-making

Off-chain decisions different stakeholders take remain in their current format. On-chain decisions are facilitated through smart contracts.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Design Principles for Blockchain-based Applications in Green Bond Reporting](../papers/green-bond-reporting-dp.md) (Ameera Darwish, Juho Lindman, Jesper Hjertqvist, Olgerta Tona, 2023).

## Related design principles

* [Design principle DP3: Smart-contract document validation](./green-bond-reporting-dp-dp3.md) - The paper states that "smart contracts facilitate the decisions that stakeholders take that can be digitized and digitalized," directly linking on-chain decision facilitation to smart-contract validation.
