# Design requirement DR2: Self-management

The solution must support patients' self-management of their own consent data, reflecting patients' rights to notice, access, and consent over the collection, use and disclosure of their personal data.

## Source paper

This design requirement is proposed by [Blockchain innovation for consent self-management in health information exchanges](../papers/consent-self-management-hie.md) (Chad Anderson, Arthur Carvalho, Mala Kaul, Jeffrey W. Merhout, 2023).
