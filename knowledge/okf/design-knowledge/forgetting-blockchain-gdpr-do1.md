# Design objective DO1: Working forgetting-blockchain prototype

Build a working prototype demonstrating that transactions can be deleted from a blockchain while maintaining functionality.

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [Design of a forgetting blockchain: A possible way to accomplish GDPR compatibility](../papers/forgetting-blockchain-gdpr.md) (Simon Farshid, Andreas Reitz, Peter Roßbach, 2019).
