# Meta-requirement MR10: Allow for domain-specific information

BEMIs should implement two domain-specific functions: a direct communication channel between buyers and sellers, and the ability for demanders to reserve suppliers' manufacturing capacities.

## Source paper

This meta-requirement is proposed by [Developing Blockchain-enabled Marketplace Interfaces: A Design Science Research Study](../papers/bemi-marketplace-interfaces.md) (Tobias Koelbel, Ahmed Zekri, Christof Weinhardt, 2023).
