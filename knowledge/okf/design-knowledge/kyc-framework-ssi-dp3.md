# Design principle DP3: Enable decentralization at the edge

Ensure that users can store their verifiable credentials on an infrastructure of their choice, supporting user autonomy and decentralization at the edge of the SSI architecture.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Designing a Framework for Digital KYC Processes Built on Blockchain-Based Self-Sovereign Identity](../papers/kyc-framework-ssi.md) (Vincent Schlatt, Johannes Sedlmeir, Simon Feulner, Nils Urbach, 2022).
