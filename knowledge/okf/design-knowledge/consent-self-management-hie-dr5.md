# Design requirement DR5: Interoperability

The solution must be interoperable across the currently fragmented health information exchange infrastructure to enable full self-management of consent, since the range of HIE organizational forms is each designed to support connectivity for only a limited set of entities.

## Source paper

This design requirement is proposed by [Blockchain innovation for consent self-management in health information exchanges](../papers/consent-self-management-hie.md) (Chad Anderson, Arthur Carvalho, Mala Kaul, Jeffrey W. Merhout, 2023).
