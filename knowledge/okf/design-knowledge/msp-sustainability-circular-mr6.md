# Meta-requirement MR6: Resource efficiency

The platform should promote resource efficiency, e.g., through smart contracts and tokenization that streamline transactions and require fewer intermediaries and resources to monitor sustainable behavior.

## Source paper

This meta-requirement is proposed by [Meta-requirements for the Design of a Blockchain-enabled Multi-sided Platform for Sustainability and Circular Economy](../papers/msp-sustainability-circular.md) (Hanna Buyssens, 2024).
