# Design requirement R5.2: Data minimization

Not only the parties involved in the KYC process but also the de facto data that are exchanged should be restricted to what is necessary, because digital data are much more comprehensive and easier to collect and abuse than their analog counterparts. We call this requirement data minimization.

## Source paper

This design requirement is proposed by [Designing a Framework for Digital KYC Processes Built on Blockchain-Based Self-Sovereign Identity](../papers/kyc-framework-ssi.md) (Vincent Schlatt, Johannes Sedlmeir, Simon Feulner, Nils Urbach, 2022).
