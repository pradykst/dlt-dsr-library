# Design requirement REQ10: Interoperability with legacy and DLT settlement systems

Interoperability with legacy private and central banking as well as private, permissioned, and permissionless DLT/blockchain and other clearing and settlement systems.

## Source paper

This design requirement is proposed by [Trading Green Bonds Using Distributed Ledger Technology](../papers/trading-green-bonds-dlt.md) (Henrik Axelsen, Ulrik Rasmussen, Johannes Rude Jensen, Omri Ross, Fritz Henglein, 2023).
