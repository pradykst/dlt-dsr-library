# Design principle DP4: Review all relevant laws

Do not only evaluate reconciliation with the GDPR but also further industry-specific laws.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Yes, I Do: Marrying Blockchain Applications with GDPR](../papers/yes-i-do-gdpr.md) (Benjamin Schellinger, Fabiane Voelter, Nils Urbach, Johannes Sedlmeir, 2022).
