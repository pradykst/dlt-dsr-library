# Design requirement DR3: Intuitive product- and health-state communication

The system should allow intuitive communication of product- and customer-health states.

## Source paper

This design requirement is proposed by [From Dissonance to Dialogue: A Token-Based Approach to Bridge the Gap Between Manufacturers and Customers](../papers/dissonance-dialogue-recall.md) (Norman Pytel, Christian Ziegler, Axel Winkelmann, 2024).
