# Meta-requirement MR8: Foster selective data transmission

BEMIs must incorporate functionalities that allow for selective data transmission to provide a secure and trustworthy environment while safeguarding the privacy and confidentiality of sensitive information.

## Source paper

This meta-requirement is proposed by [Developing Blockchain-enabled Marketplace Interfaces: A Design Science Research Study](../papers/bemi-marketplace-interfaces.md) (Tobias Koelbel, Ahmed Zekri, Christof Weinhardt, 2023).
