# Design requirement R5: Delivery history integrity and traceability

A solution must provide delivery history integrity and traceability.

## Source paper

This design requirement is proposed by [Decentralized Procurement Mechanisms for Efficient Logistics Services Mapping - a Design Science Research Approach](../papers/decentralized-procurement-logistics.md) (Tiphaine Henry, Roman Beck, Nassim Laga, Walid Gaaloul, Shenle Pan, 2022).

## Addresses

* [Design objective P3: Allocation integrity](./decentralized-procurement-logistics-do3.md) - The paper states "integrity in terms of delivery history and protocol should be considered (cf R5)."
