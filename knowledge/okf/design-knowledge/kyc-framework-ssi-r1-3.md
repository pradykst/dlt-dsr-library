# Design requirement R1.3: Standardized exchange of eKYC documents

The main barrier to cross-organizational processes is the lack of a suitable non-proprietary IT infrastructure. Thus, a standardized exchange of eKYC documents is crucial to allow for the efficient integration of eKYC checks that have been conducted at other institutions.

## Source paper

This design requirement is proposed by [Designing a Framework for Digital KYC Processes Built on Blockchain-Based Self-Sovereign Identity](../papers/kyc-framework-ssi.md) (Vincent Schlatt, Johannes Sedlmeir, Simon Feulner, Nils Urbach, 2022).
