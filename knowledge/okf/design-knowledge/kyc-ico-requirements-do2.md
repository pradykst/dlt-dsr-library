# Design objective DO2: eIDAS-compliant identity verification scheme

To ensure that all necessary identity data are collected, the KYC-system must be linked to an eIDAS compliant identity verification scheme to provide a rigorous data collection and verification process.

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [Know-Your-Customer (KYC) Requirements for Initial Coin Offerings: Toward Designing a Compliant-by-Design KYC-System Based on Blockchain Technology](../papers/kyc-ico-requirements.md) (Nadine Kathrin Ostern, Johannes Riedel, 2021).
