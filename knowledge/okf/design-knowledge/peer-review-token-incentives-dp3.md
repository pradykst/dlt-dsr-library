# Design principle DP3: Trust

Ensure the integrity, security, fairness and impartiality of the review system so that reviewers trust that their rewards and identities are protected; immutability and decentralization of blockchain address this trust principle.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Blockchain-based token system for incentivizing peer review: A design science approach](../papers/peer-review-token-incentives.md) (Chad Anderson, Pratiksha Shrestha, Suman Bhunia, Arthur Carvalho, Younghwa Lee, 2025).
