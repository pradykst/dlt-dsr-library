# Meta-requirement MR3: Tokenization

The platform should support tokenization (e.g., certifications and carbon-credit-style tokens) that can be shared in a tamper-proof fashion, bolstering confidence and avoiding greenwashing.

## Source paper

This meta-requirement is proposed by [Meta-requirements for the Design of a Blockchain-enabled Multi-sided Platform for Sustainability and Circular Economy](../papers/msp-sustainability-circular.md) (Hanna Buyssens, 2024).
