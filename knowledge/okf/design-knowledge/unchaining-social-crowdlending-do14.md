# Design objective DO14: Reduction of manual activities

The manual activities involved in the processing of the transactions and the management of project lifecycles should be further automated to reduce costs and the possibilities of fraud. Management especially mentioned cross-company activities with intermediary institutions.

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [Unchaining Social Businesses - Blockchain as the Basic Technology of a Crowdlending Platform](../papers/unchaining-social-crowdlending.md) (Andre Schweizer, Vincent Schlatt, Nils Urbach, Gilbert Fridgen, 2017).
