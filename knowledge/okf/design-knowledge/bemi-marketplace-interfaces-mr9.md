# Meta-requirement MR9: Enable customized dashboards

BEMIs must provide tabular overviews of all user-specific information in dashboards that must be specialized to supply-side and demand-side market participants.

## Source paper

This meta-requirement is proposed by [Developing Blockchain-enabled Marketplace Interfaces: A Design Science Research Study](../papers/bemi-marketplace-interfaces.md) (Tobias Koelbel, Ahmed Zekri, Christof Weinhardt, 2023).
