# Design requirement DR3: Efficient and transparent royalty payout

A decentralized DRM system for the music industry must enable efficient and transparent royalty payout, addressing massive delays and unallocated royalties.

## Source paper

This design requirement is proposed by [Blockchain-based digital rights management systems: Design principles for the music industry](../papers/drm-music-industry.md) (Raffaele Fabio Ciriello, Alexandra Cecilie Gjol Torbensen, Magnus Rotvit Perlt Hansen, Christoph Mueller-Bloch, 2023).
