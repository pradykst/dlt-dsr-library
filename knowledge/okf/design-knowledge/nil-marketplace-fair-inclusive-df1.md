# Design feature DF1: Primary Markets

Purchasing collectibles mints NFTs, and on monetary compensation a predetermined, hardcoded portion of the value is distributed as royalties among the associated student-athletes (randomized primary-market sales).

## Source paper

This design feature is a technology-specific realization proposed by [Designing a fair and inclusive digital asset-based name-image-likeness marketplace](../papers/nil-marketplace-fair-inclusive.md) (Arthur Carvalho, Liudmila Zavolokina, Suman Bhunia, Gerhard Schwabe, 2026).
