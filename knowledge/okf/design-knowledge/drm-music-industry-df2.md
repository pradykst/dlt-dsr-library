# Design feature DF2: pBFT consensus

A decentralized DRM system can validate metadata with a practical Byzantine fault tolerant consensus mechanism.

## Source paper

This design feature is a technology-specific realization proposed by [Blockchain-based digital rights management systems: Design principles for the music industry](../papers/drm-music-industry.md) (Raffaele Fabio Ciriello, Alexandra Cecilie Gjol Torbensen, Magnus Rotvit Perlt Hansen, Christoph Mueller-Bloch, 2023).
