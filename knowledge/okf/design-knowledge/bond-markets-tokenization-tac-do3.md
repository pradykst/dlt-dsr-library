# Design objective DO-3: Reduce access barriers (flexible issuance sizes and secondary market support)

The prototype should allow issuances of all sizes, reducing the barrier to consider bond issuances as a natural financing alternative, by addressing a larger group of investors. To further reduce utility thresholds, trading bonds should also be possible in secondary market options and not be restricted by business hours or geographics. Thus, asset specificity can be reduced, and subsequently, efficiency in the market increased.

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [Designing the future of bond markets: Reducing transaction costs through tokenization](../papers/bond-markets-tokenization-tac.md) (David Cisar, Benjamin Schellinger, Jens-Christian Stoetzer, Vincent Gramlich, Tobias Guggenberger, Nils Urbach, Florian Lennart Weiss, 2025).
