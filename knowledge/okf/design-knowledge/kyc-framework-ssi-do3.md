# Design objective Objective 3: Decentralization

Silos of customer data are an attractive target for attackers, and recent data breaches have significantly reduced confidence in central architectures. A viable solution for an improved eKYC process must therefore avoid central storage of customer data, and the system must be constructed to prevent lock-in effects that could result in the aggregation of market power.

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [Designing a Framework for Digital KYC Processes Built on Blockchain-Based Self-Sovereign Identity](../papers/kyc-framework-ssi.md) (Vincent Schlatt, Johannes Sedlmeir, Simon Feulner, Nils Urbach, 2022).
