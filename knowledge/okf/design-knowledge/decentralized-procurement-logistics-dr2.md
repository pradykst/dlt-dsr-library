# Design requirement R2: Autonomous allocation process

A solution must provide an autonomous allocation process.

## Source paper

This design requirement is proposed by [Decentralized Procurement Mechanisms for Efficient Logistics Services Mapping - a Design Science Research Approach](../papers/decentralized-procurement-logistics.md) (Tiphaine Henry, Roman Beck, Nassim Laga, Walid Gaaloul, Shenle Pan, 2022).

## Addresses

* [Design objective P1: Platform operating costs reduction](./decentralized-procurement-logistics-do1.md) - The paper states platform operating costs reduction "relies on an eased allocation process (cf R2)."
