# Design requirement REQ4: Guarantee ACID transaction execution

Guarantee atomic, consistent, isolated, and durable (ACID) execution of compound transactions, specifically delivery versus payment.

## Source paper

This design requirement is proposed by [Trading Green Bonds Using Distributed Ledger Technology](../papers/trading-green-bonds-dlt.md) (Henrik Axelsen, Ulrik Rasmussen, Johannes Rude Jensen, Omri Ross, Fritz Henglein, 2023).
