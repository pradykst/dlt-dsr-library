# Meta-requirement MR4: Fit transparent and interoperable reputation

A fair, transparent and interoperable reputation system should be implemented, differentiating between actor-specific trust and trust in marketplace processes.

## Source paper

This meta-requirement is proposed by [Developing Blockchain-enabled Marketplace Interfaces: A Design Science Research Study](../papers/bemi-marketplace-interfaces.md) (Tobias Koelbel, Ahmed Zekri, Christof Weinhardt, 2023).
