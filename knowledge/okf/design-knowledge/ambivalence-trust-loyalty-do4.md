# Design objective DO4: Efficiency

Enable seamless, reliable and scalable information exchange between electricity supplier and customer, minimizing blockchain data processing to retain high uptime and availability.

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [From ambivalence to trust: Using blockchain in customer loyalty programs](../papers/ambivalence-trust-loyalty.md) (Manuel Utz, Simon Johanning, Tamara Roth, Thomas Bruckner, Jens Strueker, 2023).
