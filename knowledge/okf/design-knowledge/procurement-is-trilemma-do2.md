# Design objective DO2: Stable, regulation-compliant payment processing

The artifact should embed stable and regulation-compliant payment processing into blockchain-based procurement workflows, enabling efficient and seamless value exchange among participants in both national and international contexts.

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [Designing a blockchain-based information system for procurement processes - Balancing decentralization, scalability, and security while maintaining privacy](../papers/procurement-is-trilemma.md) (Valeriya Arnold, Tobias Guggenberger, Jan Stramm, Nils Urbach, 2026).
