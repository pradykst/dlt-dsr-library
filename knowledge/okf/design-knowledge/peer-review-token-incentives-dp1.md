# Design principle DP1: Motivation-appropriate incentives

Provide incentives to reviewers that appeal to their specific motivations, since understanding and leveraging different types of motivation ensures the system effectively encourages participation.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Blockchain-based token system for incentivizing peer review: A design science approach](../papers/peer-review-token-incentives.md) (Chad Anderson, Pratiksha Shrestha, Suman Bhunia, Arthur Carvalho, Younghwa Lee, 2025).
