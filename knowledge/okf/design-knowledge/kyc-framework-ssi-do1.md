# Design objective Objective 1: Efficiency

The high cost and human resources involved in carrying out the current KYC process strongly challenges financial institutions. To allow for increased process efficiency, three requirements had to be satisfied to overcome the existing challenges: end-to-end digital processing of relevant documents, automation of manual processes, and standardized exchange of eKYC documents.

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [Designing a Framework for Digital KYC Processes Built on Blockchain-Based Self-Sovereign Identity](../papers/kyc-framework-ssi.md) (Vincent Schlatt, Johannes Sedlmeir, Simon Feulner, Nils Urbach, 2022).
