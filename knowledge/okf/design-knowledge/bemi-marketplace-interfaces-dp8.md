# Design principle DP8: Industry-specific processes for actionable decision-making

Design BEMIs that reflect industry-specific processes to guide users with actionable insights that facilitate decision making.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Developing Blockchain-enabled Marketplace Interfaces: A Design Science Research Study](../papers/bemi-marketplace-interfaces.md) (Tobias Koelbel, Ahmed Zekri, Christof Weinhardt, 2023).
