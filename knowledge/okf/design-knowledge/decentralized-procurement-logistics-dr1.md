# Design requirement R1: Allocation flexibility

A solution must provide allocation flexibility to shippers and carriers.

## Source paper

This design requirement is proposed by [Decentralized Procurement Mechanisms for Efficient Logistics Services Mapping - a Design Science Research Approach](../papers/decentralized-procurement-logistics.md) (Tiphaine Henry, Roman Beck, Nassim Laga, Walid Gaaloul, Shenle Pan, 2022).

## Addresses

* [Design objective P1: Platform operating costs reduction](./decentralized-procurement-logistics-do1.md) - The paper states platform operating costs reduction "also relies on allocation flexibility (cf R1)."
* [Design objective P2: Contractual flexibility](./decentralized-procurement-logistics-do2.md) - The paper states "the inner flexibility of the allocation mechanism ensures contractual flexibility (cf R1)."
