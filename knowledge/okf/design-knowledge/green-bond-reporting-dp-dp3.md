# Design principle DP3: Smart-contract document validation

Smart contracts can validate that an authorized party uploaded and signed the file.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Design Principles for Blockchain-based Applications in Green Bond Reporting](../papers/green-bond-reporting-dp.md) (Ameera Darwish, Juho Lindman, Jesper Hjertqvist, Olgerta Tona, 2023).

## Related design principles

* [Design principle DP5: Off-chain decision-making](./green-bond-reporting-dp-dp5.md) - The paper states that "smart contracts facilitate the decisions that stakeholders take that can be digitized and digitalized," and DP5 explicitly specifies that "on-chain decisions are facilitated through smart contracts," directly linking smart-contract validation to on-chain decision facilitation.
