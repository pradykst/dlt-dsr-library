# Meta-requirement MR1: Balance simplicity and trustworthiness

BEMI interfaces must balance simplicity and trustworthiness, ensuring usability for both tech-savvy and non-tech-savvy individuals despite the interface's overall technical complexity.

## Source paper

This meta-requirement is proposed by [Developing Blockchain-enabled Marketplace Interfaces: A Design Science Research Study](../papers/bemi-marketplace-interfaces.md) (Tobias Koelbel, Ahmed Zekri, Christof Weinhardt, 2023).
