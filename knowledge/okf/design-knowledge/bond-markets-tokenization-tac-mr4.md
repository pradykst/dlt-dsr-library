# Meta-requirement MR-4: Reduce dependency on CSDs

Every securities transaction inevitably has to use the services of Central Securities Depositories (CSDs) or delegated depository banks along the clearing and settlement chain, creating a centralized bottleneck. The prototype shall avoid such central intermediaries if possible.

## Source paper

This meta-requirement is proposed by [Designing the future of bond markets: Reducing transaction costs through tokenization](../papers/bond-markets-tokenization-tac.md) (David Cisar, Benjamin Schellinger, Jens-Christian Stoetzer, Vincent Gramlich, Tobias Guggenberger, Nils Urbach, Florian Lennart Weiss, 2025).
