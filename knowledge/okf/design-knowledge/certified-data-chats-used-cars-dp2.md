# Design principle DP2: Selective information disclosure

Allow negotiating sellers to selectively disclose information to the buyers by moving certified data to the conversation area (e.g., via drag-and-drop), enabling context-individual disclosure management.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Certified data chats for future used car markets](../papers/certified-data-chats-used-cars.md) (Andreas Engelmann, Gerhard Schwabe, 2024).
