# Design requirement DR2: Tamper-proof and automated data processing

Tamper-proof and automated data processing, for instance via smart contracts, to avoid the storage of erroneous data or its manipulation during information transfer.

## Source paper

This design requirement is proposed by [From ambivalence to trust: Using blockchain in customer loyalty programs](../papers/ambivalence-trust-loyalty.md) (Manuel Utz, Simon Johanning, Tamara Roth, Thomas Bruckner, Jens Strueker, 2023).
