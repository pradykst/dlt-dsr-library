# Design objective DO2: Social purpose

Social businesses act as a change agent for the world and pursue the creation of social benefits (Yunus et al. 2010). Thus, although the prototype is required to cover its costs, the social perspective and more cause-driven character are first priority (Boons and Lüdeke-Freund 2013).

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [Unchaining Social Businesses - Blockchain as the Basic Technology of a Crowdlending Platform](../papers/unchaining-social-crowdlending.md) (Andre Schweizer, Vincent Schlatt, Nils Urbach, Gilbert Fridgen, 2017).
