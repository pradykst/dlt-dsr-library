# Design objective DO1: Data protection

Protect data against internal and external attackers; sensitive business data and personal data must be protected from access by unauthorized third parties.

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [Striking a balance: Designing a blockchain-based solution to navigate coopetition dynamics in supply chain management](../papers/striking-balance-coopetition.md) (Jonathan Lautenschlager, Jan Stramm, Tobias Guggenberger, Nils Urbach, 2025).
