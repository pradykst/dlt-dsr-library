# Design principle DP1: Decentralized, party-independent control of the escrow/payment

The contract and its escrow/payment mechanism should be governed in a decentralized manner so that it is controlled by neither the newsvendor nor the expert; on a blockchain, escrow accounts are collectively controlled by a set of independent computational devices, which enhances trust between the parties.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Aligning the interests of newsvendors and forecasters through blockchain-based smart contracts and proper scoring rules](../papers/aligning-newsvendors-scoring-rules.md) (Arthur Carvalho, Majid Karimi, 2021).
