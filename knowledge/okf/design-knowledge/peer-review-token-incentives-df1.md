# Design feature DF1: Tokenization

Tokenization creates unique digital representations of incentives, with non-fungible and fungible tokens enabling different types of extrinsic incentive.

This design feature is a technology-specific realization proposed by [Blockchain-based token system for incentivizing peer review: A design science approach](../papers/peer-review-token-incentives.md) (Chad Anderson, Pratiksha Shrestha, Suman Bhunia, Arthur Carvalho, Younghwa Lee, 2025).
