# Design objective P1: Platform operating costs reduction

Platform operating costs reduction of the FTSP system should be considered to enhance the transportation process.

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [Decentralized Procurement Mechanisms for Efficient Logistics Services Mapping - a Design Science Research Approach](../papers/decentralized-procurement-logistics.md) (Tiphaine Henry, Roman Beck, Nassim Laga, Walid Gaaloul, Shenle Pan, 2022).
