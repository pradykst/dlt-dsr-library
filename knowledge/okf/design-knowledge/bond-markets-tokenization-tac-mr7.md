# Meta-requirement MR-7: Reduce room for opportunistic behavior

Information asymmetries can be found in many aspects of the bond token market. Potential issuers often lack financial and credit history. Further, counterparty and settlement risks exist. Also, the processes are not transparent, hindering traceability and auditability. The prototype shall reduce information asymmetries as much as possible.

## Source paper

This meta-requirement is proposed by [Designing the future of bond markets: Reducing transaction costs through tokenization](../papers/bond-markets-tokenization-tac.md) (David Cisar, Benjamin Schellinger, Jens-Christian Stoetzer, Vincent Gramlich, Tobias Guggenberger, Nils Urbach, Florian Lennart Weiss, 2025).
