# Design principle DP3: Import objects from traditional information systems

Enable the system to import objects from traditional information systems (and export unique token identifiers) to fully utilize blockchain as a leading information system for identifiers in supply networks.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Digging for Quality Management in Production Systems: A Solution Space for Blockchain Collaborations](../papers/quality-management-production.md) (Norman Pytel, Benedikt Putz, Fabian Boehm, Axel Winkelmann, 2022).
