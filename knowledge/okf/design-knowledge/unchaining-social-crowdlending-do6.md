# Design objective DO6: Define and enforce platform rules

The platform needs to be designed to act according to predefined rules (Agrawal et al. 2014). To adhere the existing standards, we follow industry regulation standards and define a maximum invest per investor as well as a maximum funding amount per student project. Furthermore, we evaluated and confirmed that assumption through the interview with one of the founders.

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [Unchaining Social Businesses - Blockchain as the Basic Technology of a Crowdlending Platform](../papers/unchaining-social-crowdlending.md) (Andre Schweizer, Vincent Schlatt, Nils Urbach, Gilbert Fridgen, 2017).
