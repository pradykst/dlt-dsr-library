# Design principle DP6: Role-based privileges

The issuer, second opinion provider, and investor are identified as different roles with different privilege rights, such as access and editing rights and building and approving blocks.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Design Principles for Blockchain-based Applications in Green Bond Reporting](../papers/green-bond-reporting-dp.md) (Ameera Darwish, Juho Lindman, Jesper Hjertqvist, Olgerta Tona, 2023).
