# Design principle DP6: Role-based privileges

Identify the issuer, second-opinion provider and investor as different roles with different privilege rights, such as access and editing rights.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Design Principles for Blockchain-based Applications in Green Bond Reporting](../papers/green-bond-reporting-dp.md) (Ameera Darwish, Juho Lindman, 2023).
