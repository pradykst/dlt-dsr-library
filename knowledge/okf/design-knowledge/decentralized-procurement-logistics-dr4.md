# Design requirement R4: CMR regulations compliance

A solution must comply with CMR regulations.

## Source paper

This design requirement is proposed by [Decentralized Procurement Mechanisms for Efficient Logistics Services Mapping - a Design Science Research Approach](../papers/decentralized-procurement-logistics.md) (Tiphaine Henry, Roman Beck, Nassim Laga, Walid Gaaloul, Shenle Pan, 2022).

## Addresses

* [Design objective P3: Allocation integrity](./decentralized-procurement-logistics-do3.md) - The paper states "the integrity of the digitalized CMR (cf R4) is paramount for regulatory authorities."
