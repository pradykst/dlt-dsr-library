# Meta-requirement MR6: Demand Side Information

The demand side must specify its request via product and production specification and an indication of its willingness to pay.

## Source paper

This meta-requirement is proposed by [Requirements and Design Principles for Blockchain-enabled Matchmaking-Marketplaces in Additive Manufacturing](../papers/matchmaking-additive-manufacturing.md) (Tobias Koelbel, Marcel Linkenheil, 2023).
