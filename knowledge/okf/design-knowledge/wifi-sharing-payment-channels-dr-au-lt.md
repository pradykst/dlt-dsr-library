# Design requirement AU#LT: Legal risks and tarnished reputation

A Wi-Fi sharing network must address the risks imposed by illegal actions of network users, which can yield losses in reputation or legal implications for hosts.

## Source paper

This design requirement is proposed by [An Architecture Using Payment Channel Networks for Blockchain-based Wi-Fi Sharing](../papers/wifi-sharing-payment-channels.md) (Christian Janiesch, Marcus Fischer, Florian Imgrund, Adrian Hofmann, Axel Winkelmann, 2023).
