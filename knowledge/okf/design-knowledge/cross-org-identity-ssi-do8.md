# Design objective DO8: Usability

The usability dimension describes how easily and intuitively the end-user can use the system. Complex graphical user interfaces and processes can lead to strong user dissatisfaction and negatively influence use. The application and handling of a certificate should be designed as intuitively as possible concerning the present use case.

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [Designing a cross-organizational identity management system: Utilizing SSI for the certification of retailer attributes](../papers/cross-org-identity-ssi.md) (Tobias Guggenberger, Daniela Kühne, Vincent Schlatt, Nils Urbach, 2023).
