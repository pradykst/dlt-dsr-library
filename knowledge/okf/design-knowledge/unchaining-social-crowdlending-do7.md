# Design objective DO7: Crowd due diligence

Research studies on the crowdfunding platform Kickstarter suggest that a greater number of perspectives available to recognize something amiss are a useful tool to detect fraud (Agrawal et al. 2014). Thus, our platform must allow for comments to a project by any user.

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [Unchaining Social Businesses - Blockchain as the Basic Technology of a Crowdlending Platform](../papers/unchaining-social-crowdlending.md) (Andre Schweizer, Vincent Schlatt, Nils Urbach, Gilbert Fridgen, 2017).
