# Design objective DO4: Infrastructure that scales with volume and users

The artifact should be built on an infrastructure capable of scaling with growing transaction volumes and users.

## Source paper

This design objective is proposed by [Designing a blockchain-based information system for procurement processes - Balancing decentralization, scalability, and security while maintaining privacy](../papers/procurement-is-trilemma.md) (Valeriya Arnold, Tobias Guggenberger, Jan Stramm, Nils Urbach, 2026).
