# Design principle DP3: Data presentation as overview and within conversation

Present certified data in used-car negotiations both as an overview and within the conversation, marking certified messages so they are recognized as certified and tamper-proof.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Certified data chats for future used car markets](../papers/certified-data-chats-used-cars.md) (Andreas Engelmann, Gerhard Schwabe, 2024).
