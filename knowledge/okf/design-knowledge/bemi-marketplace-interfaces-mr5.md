# Meta-requirement MR5: Set up adjustable visualizations

Complex and multidimensional concepts should be operationally divided into smaller parts with adjustable visualizations, allowing users to start with one particular perspective and successively take additional perspectives into account.

## Source paper

This meta-requirement is proposed by [Developing Blockchain-enabled Marketplace Interfaces: A Design Science Research Study](../papers/bemi-marketplace-interfaces.md) (Tobias Koelbel, Ahmed Zekri, Christof Weinhardt, 2023).
