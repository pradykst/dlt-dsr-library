# Design principle DP3: Consider appropriate levels of usability for customers

Customer loyalty programs should not proactively reduce access to more granular information but instead provide different levels of didactical reduction while retaining the basic message, so that customers are not disadvantaged even if they cannot understand the information at the most granular level.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [From ambivalence to trust: Using blockchain in customer loyalty programs](../papers/ambivalence-trust-loyalty.md) (Manuel Utz, Simon Johanning, Tamara Roth, Thomas Bruckner, Jens Strueker, 2023).
