# Design principle DP1: Digitization

All data is stored and exchanged digitally.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Blockchain to Rule the Waves - Nascent Design Principles for Reducing Risk and Uncertainty in Decentralized Environments](../papers/rule-the-waves-shipping.md) (Kristoffer Nærland, Christoph Müller-Bloch, Roman Beck, Søren Palmund, 2017).

[3] Source evidence: Table 4 ("Nascent Design Principles"), row "Digitization | All data is stored and exchanged digitally," article p. 11. Elaborated in the "Digitization" subsection, article pp. 11-12: "Digitization means that the loss or destruction of data becomes less likely, since the marginal cost of creating copies of data is basically zero, allowing for storing several copies. Moreover, it implies that information exchange becomes faster and more cost-effective. In addition, digitization is indispensable to enable the use of blockchain technology to manage information, and thus to facilitate tamper-proof storage of information."
