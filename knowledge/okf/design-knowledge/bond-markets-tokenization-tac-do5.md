# Design objective DO-5: Ensure regulatory compliance (with MiCAR and eWpG)

The prototype must fully comply with the legal framework in Germany. This covers both the token and specific regulatory requirements such as Know-Your-Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) checks of the investors. Subject to judicial enforcement, it must also be possible to transfer tokens even if the token holder refuses or cannot transfer for unspecified reasons. Through the newly introduced German eWpG, crypto security registers enable the issuance of bearer bonds utterly detached from physical, paper-based securities certificates. The prototype should, thus, include a crypto securities registry (CSR).

## Source paper
