# Design principle DP3: Recognize the identity holder as primary controller

Design applications so that the identity holder is an active participant in almost all processes, since all processes start with or require approval from the holder.

## Source paper

This design principle is proposed by [Designing a cross-organizational identity management system: Utilizing SSI for the certification of retailer attributes](../papers/cross-org-identity-ssi.md) (Tobias Guggenberger, Daniela Kuehne, Vincent Schlatt, Nils Urbach, 2023).
