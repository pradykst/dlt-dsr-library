# Meta-requirement MR6: Offer product and status representation

BEMIs should allow for consistent graphical representation of service operations (i.e., orders) and available hardware resources (i.e., 3D printers).

## Source paper

This meta-requirement is proposed by [Developing Blockchain-enabled Marketplace Interfaces: A Design Science Research Study](../papers/bemi-marketplace-interfaces.md) (Tobias Koelbel, Ahmed Zekri, Christof Weinhardt, 2023).
